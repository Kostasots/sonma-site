The tools

Live products from the lab.

Working in organized athletics — club, collegiate, and international — we kept spotting the same thing: places where operations could run leaner, and where the people doing the work deserved a better experience. So we started building. Field hockey is where we began, but the engine underneath — schedules, people, and judgment under pressure — fits any sport, event, or operation that runs the same way.

In development

Coordinate

Officiating operations, scheduled automatically. Upload a tournament and get conflict-aware assignments, sensible shifts, and a public schedule officials can search by name.

In testing

Umpire Assessment

Built with USA Field Hockey to replace a cumbersome workflow of juggling tabs in an Excel workbook. A field-ready assessment tool for umpire managers and evaluators: dual-umpire tracking, tap-to-tally zone maps on a real field to show an umpire exactly where problems cluster, private notes, and one-tap export — designed to make the evaluator's job faster and better. Adaptable to other sports.

Live

VU Review Log

Sticking to the FIH Video Umpire briefing script is hard under pressure — especially when English isn't the official's first language, and again in US collegiate games, where being on the clock adds its own strain. So we built a guided wizard for VUs: referral tracking, auto-generated read-aloud scripts, and one-tap email export to the umpire manager or conference supervisor. Runs on phone, tablet, or laptop.

Delivered

HR Rhythm Review

Built with and for a nurse educator. Practice and test-preparation review had lived in slides; we turned it into an interactive web app that nurses can work through at their own pace. Every scenario in it is fictional, written for practice and exam preparation.

The approach

Three points. One line from problem to proof.

Most work stalls in the gap between a strategy and a working thing. We close it.

Frame

We map the real problem, not the symptom — the constraints, the people, and the way work actually moves, before anyone reaches for a fix.

Solve

Then we design the fix that fits. Sometimes that's software we build ourselves; sometimes it's a process change, the right vendor, or a partnership. We're loyal to the outcome, not to any one kind of solution.

Prove

And we measure it. If it doesn't move a number that matters to you, it isn't finished. Impact is the deliverable, not the report.
